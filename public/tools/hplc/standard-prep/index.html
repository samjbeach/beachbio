Check before you trust the numbers. The A1%1cm values default to common literature figures (astaxanthin ≈2100; β-apo-8′-carotenal ≈2640) but are solvent- and wavelength-specific — overwrite them with the value for your solvent and detection wavelength, or your whole curve is rescaled by the ratio of the error. The dilution factor is the dilution you applied to the primary stock before reading its absorbance (set to 1 if you read it neat).
