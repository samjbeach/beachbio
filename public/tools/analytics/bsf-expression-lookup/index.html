TPM is gene-level — shared across a gene's isoforms. Use the filters to focus on a stage or tissue; “collapse replicates” averages biological replicates (e.g. the four midgut samples) to a mean ± SEM.

How this is built. The annotation is transcript-level, so genes with multiple isoforms (.t1, .t2…) show one annotation block per isoform — their BLAST hits and domains can differ. TPM is measured per gene and shown once. Data is joined offline by prep.py into data.parquet and queried in your browser with DuckDB-WASM.
