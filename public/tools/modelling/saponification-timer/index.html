Defatting clock · second-order saponification

When is the BSF reaction defatted enough to quench?

A sequential ester-hydrolysis model (TAG→DAG→MAG→glycerol, each step second-order in [OH⁻]) that tracks neutral lipid leaving the organic phase. The absolute rate constant for this exact system isn't in the literature, so calibrate it against one TLC timepoint — then the curve becomes a real predictor for re-scaling lipid, base, volume or temperature.

Presets seed the two defensible bounds: the protocol's own ~8 h expectation vs. homogeneous FAME-in-ethanol kinetics (Lim & Harvey). They differ ~17×. Treat the true value as unknown until calibrated.

Calibrate from TLC

Read "neutral lipid left" off the high-Rf TAG/DAG band intensity vs. the t=0 lane (densitometry or eyeball). One point fixes kᵦ; a second point validates the order.
