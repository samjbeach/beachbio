Read this before trusting the numbers. This is a relative, qualitative model for building intuition and ranking bottlenecks — not for predicting titre. Key insect-specific assumptions baked in: no squalene/sterol sink (the dominant yeast drain — insects lack it); native MVA throughput is low (default 0.1× yeast); the MVA pathway only reaches FPP natively; and IDI activity is unknown and is the highlighted key tunable. Most kinetic constants are placeholders extrapolated from yeast/Drosophila. Validation: load preset 1 (yeast) and confirm lycopene accumulates under strong GGPPS + WT cyclase, then switch the cyclase to Y27R (or lower SaGGPPs) and watch β-carotene flux recover.
