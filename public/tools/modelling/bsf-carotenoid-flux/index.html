BSF carotenoid pathway model

A relative, steady-state map of the isoprenoid → carotenoid pathway in black soldier fly (Hermetia illucens) fat body. Build a construct by switching enzymes on, expressing endogenous ones recombinantly, and grouping them into cassettes — then see where flux gets stuck and which enzyme is worth boosting next. Relative units, for prioritising experiments — not a titre prediction.

Where's the leverage? — boost which enzyme to make more β-carotene?

Bars show how much the end-product flux moves if you boost each enzyme 1% (a flux-control coefficient) at the midpoint parameter set. Long green bar = high leverage, boost it. Red = boosting it hurts (it's pulling flux into a side-branch). Near-zero = not limiting right now.

Read-out

2Construct

Endogenous enzymes are already in the fly — their native activity is feedback-limited. Put an enzyme in a cassette to express it recombinantly (cisgenic, semi-synthetic promoter — escapes transcriptional feedback). Exception: active-site product inhibition (e.g. MVK by GPP/FPP/GGPP) is not escaped by a strong promoter — only a feedback-resistant (FBR) variant escapes it. Heterologous enzymes only exist once they're in a cassette. An enzyme can sit in several cassettes at once (its expression is the sum of those promoters).

Cassettes — co-expressed groups; one promoter strength each

Endogenous enzymes — native (feedback-limited) ± a recombinant copy

Heterologous enzymes — added by you

Feedstock (isopentenol / IUP bypass)

BSF has no native route from isopentenols to the pathway — feeding does nothing until you add the IUP module (choline kinase + IPK) to a cassette in the heterologous table. Prenol then primes DMAPP directly, sidestepping weak IDI. Isopentenols can be toxic; drop viability per your own data.
